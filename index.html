01 • FORESTS

The Green Heart of Earth

Forests are among Earth's most important ecosystems. They provide habitats, regulate climate, protect soil and support millions of people.

🌲 Ancient Forest

Why Forests Matter

Forests cover a significant portion of Earth's land and provide shelter to an extraordinary variety of species. They absorb carbon dioxide, release oxygen and help regulate temperature and rainfall.

🌱 Clean AirTrees help improve air quality.
💧 Water CycleForests influence rainfall and water flow.
🐒 Wildlife HabitatMillions of species depend on forests.
🌍 Climate SupportForests store carbon.

02

10 • POLLUTION

Keep Our Planet Clean

Pollution can affect air, water, soil and living organisms. Understanding its sources helps us find better solutions.

🏭

Air Pollution

Harmful gases and particles can enter the atmosphere through transport, industry and other activities.

Solution: Cleaner transport and energy.
🌊

Water Pollution

Waste, chemicals and plastics can damage rivers, lakes, oceans and aquatic life.

Solution: Reduce waste and protect waterways.
🗑️

Land Pollution

Improper waste disposal can contaminate soil and harm ecosystems.

Solution: Reduce, reuse and recycle.
